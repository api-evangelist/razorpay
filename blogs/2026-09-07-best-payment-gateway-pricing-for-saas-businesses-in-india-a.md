The best payment gateway for SaaS businesses in India is the one that delivers the lowest Total Cost of Ownership, not the one with the lowest headline MDR. TCO includes transaction fees, payment success rates, subscription recovery, and fixed costs like AMC and setup charges.. It happens at month-end.
