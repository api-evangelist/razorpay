Video KYC for payment gateway onboarding in India is a live, consent-based identity check. If your application asks for a video call after you enter business and PAN details, you may wonder who should attend and whether CKYC can remove the step. RBI calls its regulated remote identification process V-CIP, or Video-based Customer Identification Process.
