Starting October 1, 2026, a significant regulatory update will come into effect concerning the Export Declaration Form (EDF) for all exporters. This new requirement, stemming from FEMA regulations, aims to streamline export compliance. While goods exporters have long been familiar with the EDF process, this update extends the mandatory filing to services and software exporters.
