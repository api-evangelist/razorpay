Payment gateway pricing can include transaction-based charges, optional service charges, and applicable taxes. These charges are not always labelled consistently across providers, which can make payment costs difficult to compare. This guide explains MDR, TDR, platform fees, technology fees, setup fees, and annual maintenance charges.
