Indian Shopify merchants asking “what does Razorpay cost” are asking the wrong question. The headline transaction rate (TDR) is only one line in a longer bill. The real number is total cost of ownership (TCO): the transaction rate plus annual maintenance charges (AMC), setup fees, and revenue lost to failed payments.
