The PF wage ceiling has changed for the first time in more than a decade. From 17 September 2026, the wage ceiling for mandatory EPFO coverage has increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month. If you manage payroll, you’re probably wondering: Who does this affect, and what do I need to change?
